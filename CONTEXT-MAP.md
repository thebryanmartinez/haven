# Context Map

## Contexts

- [Pockets](./apps/pockets/CONTEXT.md) — subdivides one bank account into funds and tracks transactions inside each fund
- [Tally](./apps/tally/CONTEXT.md) — tracks net worth over time from assets and liabilities

## Relationships

- **Pockets ↔ Tally**: none. A Tally bank asset does not read from Pockets; the user enters its valuations by hand.
